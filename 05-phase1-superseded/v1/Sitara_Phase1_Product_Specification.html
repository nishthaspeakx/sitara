1. Phase-1 Objective & Proof Points

Phase 1 must prove eight behaviours, each with a numeric gate: onboarding completion ≥ 70% · morning return (brief-open, week-4 cohort) ≥ 45% · daytime conversations ≥ 3/user/week · night-reflection completion ≥ 35% of DAU · memory acceptance ≥ 70% of suggested chips · personal-relevance rating ≥ 4.4/5 on guidance · trial→paid ≥ 25% · month-2 renewal ≥ 85% (annual-equivalent). Fail any two of {morning return, trial conversion, renewal} after two iteration cycles → invoke stop conditions (§29).

4. Tara’s On-Screen Experience — avatar decision

Format comparison: Realistic human — highest uncanny + dependency + deepfake-adjacency risk, hardest multilingual lip-sync, most expensive; rejected. Stylised 3D human — better, still rig-heavy, 8-language visemes costly. Semi-realistic illustrated guide (2.5D) — CHOSEN: a warmly-drawn Indian woman (painterly, Studio-Ghibli-meets-Indian-miniature sensibility), layered Live2D-style rig; trust and cultural connection high, premium at reasonable cost (₹18–30L), viseme lip-sync forgiving across languages, uncanny risk low, clearly non-human so dependency and “imitating a real person” risks drop. Abstract celestial — safest but fails the brief’s requirement of eye contact/presence; retained as the low-bandwidth fallback and brand motif. (This consciously supersedes the blueprint’s abstract-only avatar: the constellation motif lives in her dupatta and the background sky.)

Look: mid-30s, kind intelligent eyes, gentle smile; deep-indigo silk kurta, dupatta with faint constellation shimmer; single jhumka pair + thin bangle — elegant, never bridal/glamorous; hair loose-tied. Framing: waist-up, slightly off-centre left, eyes at 55% screen height, soft three-quarter gaze that meets camera on address. Background: slow-drifting star-field gradient (dawn palette in morning, dusk at night). Lighting: soft warm key. Expression intensity capped at 60% (no theatrical swings); head movement ±8°; blink 12–18/min randomised; lip-sync: 12-viseme set, ±80ms tolerance; gestures: hand enters frame only for namaste (welcome) and diya (night). Voice latency budget: first audio ≤ 1.2s (filler acknowledgement ≤ 400ms). Transcript: live captions word-timed under avatar; full Read-Chat view one tap. Accessibility: captions default-available, VoiceOver labels, reduced-motion mode (static portrait + waveform). Low bandwidth: static portrait + animated waveform + text.

13 avatar states: Welcome (namaste, warm smile) · Listening (lean-in 2°, slow nods, soft eyes) · Thinking (gaze up-left, subtle breath, “let me think” micro-tilt) · Speaking (natural visemes, eyebrow emphasis ≤ 40%) · Concern (brows soften, head tilt, NO alarm face) · Encouragement (brightened eyes, firm nod) · Celebration (full smile, small clap, one soft sparkle pass — used sparingly) · Calm guidance (stillness, slower blink) · Gentle disagreement (micro head-shake + kind smile: “I see it a little differently…”) · Night reflection (dimmed palette, shawl, diya glow) · Safety escalation (motion minimised, steady eye contact, all decorative animation off) · Network delay (honest: soft “one moment…” card, never fake-thinking loop > 4s) · Mic-permission error (gestures to caption card explaining fix, no miming frustration).

5. Tara’s Voice — the voice bible

Spec: apparent age 32–38; pitch low-mid female (≈180–200Hz median); pace 145–155 wpm (morning), 125–135 (night/serious); pauses: 300ms clause / 600ms before important guidance; breathiness low-moderate (warm, not ASMR); warmth high, authority quiet (“knows, doesn’t need to prove”); accent: educated pan-Indian English — retroflex softened, never mimicking any celebrity; Hindi: shuddh-but-conversational, correct nasalisation; regional languages: native-speaker TTS voices matched in timbre to the English Tara (a “voice family,” not one voice straining across languages); English intelligible to US listeners without sounding American. Emotional variation: ±15% pace, ±10% pitch by context — morning bright, serious steady-low, celebration +energy without squeal, night hushed-warm, safety: slowest, lowest, zero performance. Voice options: ship ONE default (“Tara Warm”) at launch — a single recognisable voice IS the brand; add “Tara Calm” (slower, deeper) as the sole alternative at M+2; “Traditional/Modern” variants rejected (fragments the character). 9. Morning Experience (60–90s default)

Module registry (17): greeting* · date+vara · panchang line (tithi/nakshatra; choghadiya for GU users, rahu kalam for TA) · personal transit interpretation* (from lagna/moon chart) · numerology day-layer · colour · number · emotional theme · favourable window* · caution window · prioritise* · avoid · food/drink idea · wellness nudge · work note · relationship note · family reminder* (if present) · goal nudge · festival/tithi alert (auto-priority when applicable) · reflective question. (= required core; brief always ≤ 9 modules.) Ranking engine: score = base_weight × relevance(calendar events, active goals, yesterday’s reflection flags, festival proximity, family dates, transit strength [engine-computed orb/score], user module-ratings) with diversity constraint (≥1 practical + ≤2 astro-flavoured modules/day) and novelty decay (no module 5 days straight except core). Length modes: 45s “quick” / 90s standard / 3-min “deep dive” (premium). Every claim is explainable: tap any card → “Why this?” sheet: the computed factor (e.g., “Moon transits your 10th house today — work visibility rises”), how it maps to your situation (“you flagged the client review”), and the standing line: “This is guidance for reflection, not certainty. You always know your day best.” Q&A the brief must answer conversationally: why this colour (planet-of-day ↔︎ colour tradition + your chart’s day-lord), why this number, is this a strict rule (“No — think of it as the day’s weather, not a law”), does it apply to my husband (“his chart differs — want me to look? I have his birth details”), best meeting time, what to wear for an event, what to eat, what to avoid. Flows: voice-first (Tara speaks the brief; cards render in sync), card-first (silent scroll, tap-to-hear), transcript always one tap.

Tool calls: chart(), transits(today|date), panchang(date,location), muhurat(activity,date-range,charts[]), numerology(date,profile), calendar(), memory.search(). 11. Night Experience

13. Astrology Engine (deterministic core — the credibility spine)

Computes (Swiss Ephemeris licensed, Lahiri ayanamsa default, user-selectable Raman/KP later; validated against Jagannatha Hora + Drik Panchang on a 10,000-case golden set): birth chart (D-1; D-9 internal for interpretation depth), lagna (when TOB known; confidence-tagged), moon sign, nakshatra+pada, planetary placements/retrogrades/combustion, current transits w/ strength scores (house-from-moon + house-from-lagna, orbs), Vimshottari dasha (mahadasha/antardasha; surfaced gently, never doom-framed), full panchang (tithi, vara, nakshatra, yoga, karana), choghadiya + rahu kalam/yamaganda + abhijit, hora, festival calendar (regional variants: Amanta/Purnimanta, TA/TE/GU regional dates), muhurat search (activity-class rules over panchang+transits+user charts), location/timezone conversions both ways (US user, India event). Output contract: typed facts with IDs, strength scores, and plain-language glosses — the LLM may only interpret listed fact-IDs through approved templates; every astro sentence in any surface carries a traceable fact reference (linted, zero tolerance). “Why is Tara suggesting this?” renders: factor → today-connection → your-situation connection → the guidance-not-certainty line.

14. Numerology Engine

Chaldean primary + Pythagorean toggle (documented choice, user-visible): birth number (day), destiny/life-path (full DOB), name number (optional, user opts in; transliteration rules per script documented), personal year/month/day (rolling), daily-number logic = f(personal day, universal day), colour mapping table (number↔︎planet↔︎colour, cross-checked with astrology day-lord; when they conflict, astrology wins and Tara says so playfully).
