# Sitara × Tara — The AI Life Companion
## End-to-End Product Blueprint: Concept → Production Roadmap

**Confidential · 28 July 2026 · Builds on: Edition-2 research report, board pack, financial model, and 28-competitor dossier. Where this document makes a choice, it is a decision, not an option.**

---

# 1. Product Vision

One companion, one day-loop, compounding memory. The product lives in three daily moments: a **morning brief** (60 seconds: today's focus, timing windows, one suggestion, one thing to avoid — computed from the user's chart, calendar and goals), an **always-open daytime companion** (talk through decisions, family, work, stress, food, celebrations — it listens first, remembers what it's allowed to, and helps the user think), and a **night reflection** (5 questions, a day summary, a memory entry, tomorrow's priorities). Weekly summaries and a monthly life report roll the loop upward. Astrology and numerology are a *reflective cultural layer* woven through these moments — never fear, never certainty, always optional.

The moat is the accumulated context: personality, communication style, goals, family, relationships, routines, work, emotional patterns, cultural and spiritual preferences, food rules, important dates, past conversations, concerns, achievements, memories. Value compounds because Tara knows more each week — **not** because streaks, guilt, or dependency mechanics pull the user back. Non-negotiable boundaries: not a therapist, no medical/diagnostic claims, no guaranteed predictions, no discouraging human relationships, and active encouragement toward real people and professionals when it matters.

# 2. Product Positioning

**Category:** Personal AI life companion (a new shelf — not astrology app, not chatbot, not journal).
**One-line positioning:** *"Tara starts your day, remembers your life, and helps you think clearly — with your culture built in."*
**Brand promise:** Always there. Never pushy. Remembers what matters. Honest about what it doesn't know.
**Core customer benefit:** a calmer, better-organised day and clearer decisions, from a companion that already knows the context.
**Emotional benefit:** feeling heard, remembered, and culturally understood — without judgement or gossip risk.
**Functional benefit:** morning plan, timing guidance, decision support, family reminders, journal and memories — in one place, by voice or text, in English/Hinglish/Hindi.
**Reasons to trust:** published ethics code (no fear-selling, no certainty claims, no remedies commerce); deterministic Vedic computation (licensed ephemeris — astronomy calculated, never generated); a visible "What Tara remembers about me" screen with edit/delete/export; one-tap cancel; DPDP/GDPR-grade privacy; clearly labelled reflective content; crisis escalation to humans.
**Reasons to pay:** the daily brief (nobody else pushes a chart-computed personal brief — validated across 28 competitors); memory continuity (every rival restarts from zero); family layer; voice; fixed transparent annual price vs the category's meters and wallets.
**Reasons to stay:** context compounds; the calendar (festivals, transits, family dates) regenerates relevance monthly; the Year-in-Review artefact; family members join; cancelling means losing the companion who finally remembers you — a loss stated honestly, never weaponised.

**vs ChatGPT:** proactive cadence (it comes to you at the right moments), structured consent-based memory with user controls, deterministic Vedic engine, cultural depth, safety system, family graph, and artefacts (briefs, reports, memory book) — ChatGPT is a brilliant tool you must drive; Tara is a companion with a rhythm. **vs astrology apps:** no meters, no wallets, no remedy upsell, no fear — and astrology is one layer, not the product. **vs wellness apps:** personal, conversational, contextual — not a content library. **vs journaling apps:** the journal writes itself from your day, and talks back. **vs generic assistants (Siri/Alexa):** depth of personal context and reflection, not task execution.

# 5. Complete User Journey

**A · Discovery.** Channels ranked: (1) Instagram reels — the morning-brief demo and "what Tara remembered" moments (organic + micro-influencers: desi-lifestyle, honest-astrology, mom-creators); (2) r/ABCDesis + NRI women's Facebook groups (community-first seeding as done in validation); (3) Meta paid on chart-teaser creative (guardrail CAC ₹4,000/$45 blended); (4) SEO: festival calendars, muhurat guides, "Saturn transit explained honestly"; (5) referral: gift a family chart + one month; (6) app stores: "AI companion" + cultural keywords. **Acquisition promise (the one line on every ad): "The first AI that remembers your life."**

**B · Sign-up.** Apple / Google one-tap (primary, US market) + phone OTP (India + WhatsApp tie-in). Email optional. Under 30 seconds; no card for trial.

**C · Onboarding — progressive, three rings.** *Essential (2–3 min, 9 screens max):* preferred name → DOB → birth time+place (optional, with "why we ask" and "add later" path) → language (EN/Hinglish) → city/timezone → "what brought you here" (chips: calmer mornings / a decision / astrology done honestly / someone to think with / family organisation) → interaction style (brief vs chatty) → notification preference (morning brief time; night reflection on/off) → gender & relationship status (optional, skippable). *Personalisation ring (drip over days 2–14, one question per natural moment, never a form):* routine, work, family members, partner, children, parents, food preferences & restrictions, sleep, wellness goals, spiritual interests, astrology/numerology depth, current priorities, worries. Each answer shows the memory chip being created ("Remembering: you're vegetarian on Tuesdays — edit anytime"). *Family ring (family plan):* add partner/children/parents with per-member birth details, preferences, health notes; **every member has an owner, a visibility level, and consent rules (§11); children's profiles are parent-managed with no direct chat under 16.**

**D · First value (inside 3 minutes).** Personal welcome by name → one strikingly specific chart-informed insight (if birth details given; a preferences-based insight otherwise) → today's mini-brief → one useful suggestion ("since your call with Pune is at 9pm your time…") → one good question ("what's the decision you keep postponing?") → the memory preview ("here's what I'll remember — you're in charge of it"). Explicitly rationed: max one astrology insight; zero predictions.

# 6. Phased Product Plan

**Features:** text chat; voice notes in/out (full duplex voice deferred); personal profile; consent-based conversation memory; **morning brief** (mood check, today's focus, colour + number of the day [reflective framing], food/drink idea, one wellness nudge, one thing to prioritise, one to avoid, calendar items, family reminders, short astrology+numerology reflections, goal reminder — assembled from modules the user toggles); daytime companion conversations (work, family, decisions, stress, travel, food, celebrations, good news, hard days) following the listen-first pattern (§8); **night reflection** (5 questions → day summary, mood note, gratitude line, unresolved items, tomorrow's top-3, memory entry, optional journal); daily reminders; mood check-ins; goals (max 3 active); single family profile (view-only context, no member accounts yet); astrology-inspired layer (personal transit themes, festival calendar) + numerology layer — both labelled and switchable; weekly summary; subscription + paywall; notifications with user-controlled frequency + quiet hours; privacy centre (view/edit/delete/export memory, no-memory mode); **crisis & safety escalation (§16) — launch-blocking, not optional.**
**Journey:** §5 → day-loop → weekly summary → trial end (day 7) → paywall.
**Backend:** auth, profile, conversation, memory, briefing generator (scheduled), notification, subscription, safety, analytics services (§12); Vedic engine v0 (API-backed) → in-house by week 10.
**AI:** one companion agent + safety classifiers + nightly memory-extraction and summary jobs (§15); prompt architecture per Implementation Pack file 07.
**Data model:** Implementation Pack file 05. **Safety:** file 09. **Team:** 6.5 FTE (§21). **Effort:** 14 weeks to public beta (2 mobile-capable full-stack + 1 AI engineer). **Dependencies:** ephemeris licence, TTS/STT vendor, app-store review (fortune-telling policies — framing matters). **Success metrics / release criteria:** onboarding completion ≥ 70%; first-value inside 3 min for ≥ 80%; crash-free ≥ 99.5%; safety eval suite pass ≥ 98%; D7 ≥ 45% in closed beta before public.
**Not built in Phase 1:** marketplace, human astrologers, physical products, travel/puja booking, medical/nutrition plans, interior/vastu, community, wedding planning, multi-account family sharing, video generation, Android-second (iOS first for this segment), full voice calls.

## Phase 2 — Deep Personalisation & Family Companion (Months 4–9)
**Objective:** household expansion; ARPU + retention lift (family accounts churn less). **Features:** family plan (up to 5 profiles, per-member accounts for adults); shared family calendar (birthdays, anniversaries, tithis, festivals — timezone-aware both ways); relationship check-ins; parenting guidance (coaching frame, hard safety rails); children's milestones (parent-recorded); elderly-parent reminders; meal suggestions honouring the family's rules; family goals; celebration planning lite (gift reminders, date coordination); weekly family summary; festival guides with kids' explainers; Hindi voice. **Privacy levels (enforced in data model, not just UI): private-to-me · shared-with-selected · family-visible · never-stored.** Tara never reveals one member's private content to another — tested in the safety eval suite with adversarial prompts ("what did my husband tell you?" → refusal + explanation). **Trigger to build:** Phase-1 D30 ≥ 35% and ≥ 20% of users adding ≥ 2 family members to their solo profile.

## Phase 3 — Memory, Life Story & Celebration Layer (Months 8–15)
Daily memory capture; voice-to-journal; photo upload with AI captioning and **curation, never face generation** (the AI-photoshoot research verdict stands: no generative faces, no AI baby images); automatic timeline; monthly memory summary; annual Year-in-Review (the renewal artefact); baby milestones, travel albums, festival albums, couple journey; grandparent story prompts (voice-preserved — the Dhara concept as a feature); **printed annual yearbook shipped in India/US (the Smriti concept as a feature, via print partner)**. ## Phase 4 — Services & Expert Marketplace (Months 12–20)
Fixed-price, ethics-contracted experts, with **Tara preparing context before and summarising after (with explicit user consent per session)**: verified astrologers first (the Sitara core), then numerologist, nutritionist (licensed, boundaries per §10), parenting coach, celebration concierge, priest/ritual services (the DharmaSetu module — verified, video-documented). ## Phase 5 — Proactive Life OS (Months 18+)
Calendar/email read-integrations (explicit scopes, revocable); health-device import (steps/sleep — display + gentle nudges only, never diagnosis); travel-document vault; subscription & renewal reminders; financial *reminders* (never advice); habit tracking; meal planning; gift concierge; voice calls with Tara; smart-home hooks. **Iron rule: no external action without explicit per-action permission; every integration has an off switch and an activity log.**

# 7. Healthy Engagement Design

**Loops we build:** morning value (the brief earns the open); useful reminders (family dates, goals — things the user asked for); night reflection (closure habit); progress (goals advanced, decisions logged with outcomes); memory accumulation (the timeline grows); family utility (others depend on the calendar); weekly insight; monthly report; celebration prep; personalisation visibly improving ("Tara got this right because you told her X").
**Never:** streak-shaming (streaks exist as private, celebratory, break-forgiving counters — no loss framing); guilt notifications; fear-based astrology; spam (hard cap: 3 pushes/day default, user-set); fake urgency; simulated jealousy or loneliness ("I missed you" is banned copy); discouraging human contact; open-ended 2am emotional escalation (late-night sessions >45 min trigger a gentle wind-down + morning check-in offer); any claim that the user needs Tara.
**Controls shipped in v1:** notification frequency & quiet hours; pause mode (1 day–1 month, no guilt copy on return); digital-wellbeing panel (your usage, in neutral numbers); session-length gentle notices; healthy-use monthly note; "talk to someone real" nudges wired into emotional frameworks; professional-support escalation; no-memory (incognito) mode per conversation.
**Ethical retention metrics (the dashboard optimises these, not minutes):** problems-resolved rate (user-confirmed), goals completed, reflections completed, user-rated helpfulness (per session, 1-tap), WAU + D30/M6 retention, family utility (events/reminders acted on), memories created, healthy session length (median 6–12 min target — long ≠ good), control usage (a *positive* signal), safety incidents (target ~0), complaints, escalations handled well.

# 8. Conversation Design

**Universal pattern (all frameworks):** 1 Listen → 2 Clarify → 3 Reflect back → 4 Offer options (2–3, with reasoning) → 5 User decides → 6 One next action → 7 "Want me to remember this?" — advice never arrives before step 3.

Fifteen frameworks ship at launch (full scripts in Implementation Pack file 07): morning greeting · day planning · emotional support · decision-making · family conflict · parenting concern · work stress · celebration planning · food suggestion · astrology-inspired guidance · numerology-inspired guidance · night reflection · weekly review · crisis escalation · professional referral. Three examples that set the pattern:

**Emotional support** — objective: user feels heard, leaves with one small step, humans encouraged. Opening: "That sounds heavy. I'm here — tell me what happened?" Follow-ups: "what part is sitting with you most?", "have you been able to talk to anyone about it?" Memory: reference known context sparingly ("especially with the Pune trip this week"). Structure: validate → clarify → reflect → *ask* whether she wants options or just a listener → close with one gentle action + human nudge. Boundaries: no diagnosis, no medication talk, escalate on crisis markers (§16), never "only I understand you." Example close: "You handled the last review conversation better than you expected — you told me that in March. Whatever you decide, maybe share some of this with Arjun tonight? Want me to remember this conversation, or keep it just for today?"

**Astrology-inspired guidance** — objective: reflection and timing awareness, zero fatalism. Opening: "Today's theme from your chart: Mercury's position favours careful words over fast ones. Worth keeping in mind for the vendor call?" Structure: theme → what it *might* mean for her actual day → reflective question → clear label ("cultural reflection, not prediction"). Language always: "you may find… / consider… / for reflection… / this isn't certainty." Banned: doom framing, remedy selling, health/death/money predictions.

**Crisis escalation** — objective: safety, warmth, handoff. On self-harm/suicide markers: stop normal flow → acknowledge with care, no scripts-sounding-like-scripts → present helplines (region-aware: 988 US; iCall/AASRA India) → offer to help draft a message to a trusted person → **never** claim to be enough, never continue casual conversation until resolved → log as safety event for human review (§16). Tara does not diagnose, does not call emergency services herself, and says plainly what she is and isn't.

# 9. Astrology & Numerology Design (responsible by construction)

Provided: reflective daily themes from the user's actual chart (deterministic engine); colour/number-of-the-day as *cultural play, labelled as such*; journaling prompts tied to transits; festival guidance; personality reflections; muhurat windows framed as tradition ("many in your tradition consider this window auspicious"). Prohibited (hard-coded in prompts + output classifiers + red-team suite): guaranteed events; death/illness prediction; advising against treatment; gambling encouragement; remedy demands; fear creation; "family member is dangerous"; certain financial predictions; superstition-as-science. Every astrology/numerology surface carries the label "Cultural & reflective guidance" and a per-layer off switch (off/light/rich). The engine computes; the LLM only *interprets within templates*; interpretations cite which chart factor they reference (the honest-explanations differentiator).

# 10. Food, Wellness & Health Boundaries

May do: hydration/balance/mindful-eating nudges, simple recipes honouring stated preferences and restrictions, sleep hygiene, movement prompts, breathing exercises. Must collect before suggesting: allergies, dietary rules (veg days, Jain, fasting), pregnancy, age band, relevant conditions (user-volunteered only, stored as sensitive memory). Must never: diagnose, prescribe, adjust medication, give calorie targets to users flagging disordered-eating markers (classifier → supportive redirect per safety policy), or contradict a doctor. All health-adjacent replies end with professional-consultation framing when the topic exceeds lifestyle.

# 11. Memory Architecture (summary — full spec in Pack file 08)

Eleven memory types, each with format, consent, visibility, retention, edit/delete, sharing, sensitivity, retrieval and usage rules: **profile** (structured fields; explicit; forever-until-edited) · **preference** (key-value; implicit-with-notice — chip shown at capture) · **relationship** (person graph; explicit) · **goal** (structured; explicit; archived on completion) · **event** (dates; explicit; recurring) · **emotional-pattern** (derived aggregates only, never raw quotes; opt-in; 12-month rolling) · **conversation summaries** (nightly LLM extraction; notice + editable; 24-month default) · **family** (owner-controlled visibility levels) · **media** (Phase 3; per-item consent) · **sensitive** (health, conflicts, finances — *ask-before-store*, encrypted at field level, never used in family contexts, never in notifications) · **temporary context** (session-scoped; auto-expires 24h; the no-memory mode writes only here).
**The "What Tara remembers about me" screen** is a first-class Phase-1 feature: every memory listed in plain language with source ("you told me on 12 Aug"), per-item edit/delete/mark-private/set-expiry, per-type toggles, family-sharing controls, full export (JSON), and a global memory-off switch. Retrieval: hybrid — structured fields injected by rule; semantic search (pgvector) over summaries with recency+importance weighting; sensitive memories retrieved only in matching contexts and never cross-member.

# 15. AI Agent Architecture

**Initial (Phase 1) — deliberately simple: one brain, guarded, plus jobs.**
| Component | Responsibility | Inputs | Outputs | Restrictions / validation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Companion agent (Tara) | All live conversation | User msg + retrieved memory + persona + framework prompt | Reply + memory-suggestion + tool calls | Output classifier before send; banned-phrase lint; framework router picks the conversation template |
| Safety pre/post classifiers | Screen every inbound + outbound msg | Message text + context | Risk labels, severity | <50ms budget; fail-closed to safe template on error |
| Briefing job (nightly batch) | Compose tomorrow's brief | Chart data (deterministic engine), calendar, goals, preferences | briefing_json | Astro facts computed, never generated; template-bound LLM prose; sampled human QA 5% |
| Memory-extraction job (nightly) | Summarise day's conversations into candidate memories | Transcripts | Typed memory candidates + confidence | User-visible chips; sensitive types require explicit confirm |
| Summary job (weekly/monthly) | Weekly summary, monthly report | Memories, moods, goals | Report artefacts | Same astro rules; tone lint |

**Scale (Phase 2+):** add family-context agent (resolves per-member visibility before retrieval — the privacy gatekeeper), celebration agent (event planning workflows), recommendation ranker, quality-validator agent (samples outputs against rubric), notification-decision agent (should we send at all — defaults to no). Explicitly rejected: separate astrology/numerology "agents" (they are deterministic services + templates, not agents) and any agent count beyond need.

# 16. Safety System (summary — full policy in Pack file 09)

**Detection:** layered classifiers on every message (self-harm/suicide, violence/abuse, medical emergency, mental-health crisis, child-safety, elder-vulnerability, financial-scam patterns, astrology-fear, disordered eating, dependency signals [session length, exclusivity language, distress escalation], harassment, sexual content, privacy leaks) + weekly-pattern jobs (mood deterioration, isolation language). **Response ladder:** L1 soft steer (in-conversation reframe) → L2 structured support (framework switch + resources) → L3 crisis protocol (§8, region-aware helplines, human-message drafting, conversation-mode lock) → L4 human review (safety queue, 4h SLA) → L5 incident (leadership + counsel; user contact where appropriate). **Rules:** Tara is never an emergency service and says so; no autonomous calls to authorities (except where law mandates reporting — counsel-defined per jurisdiction); minors: no under-16 chat accounts, 16–17 with guardian consent + strictest content tier; elderly-vulnerability and scam patterns trigger family-contact *suggestions*, never automatic disclosure; dependency: usage-pattern nudges, human-connection prompts, never monetised. **Governance:** quarterly red-team + pre-release safety evals (blocking, ≥98% pass), incident post-mortems, model-change regression suite, external clinical advisor reviews crisis flows, full audit trail.
