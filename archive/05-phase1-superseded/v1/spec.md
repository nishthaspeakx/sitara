# 4. Tara's On-Screen Experience — avatar decision
**Format comparison:** Realistic human — highest uncanny + dependency + deepfake-adjacency risk, hardest multilingual lip-sync, most expensive; rejected. Stylised 3D human — better, still rig-heavy, 8-language visemes costly. **Semi-realistic illustrated guide (2.5D) — CHOSEN**: a warmly-drawn Indian woman (painterly, Studio-Ghibli-meets-Indian-miniature sensibility), layered Live2D-style rig; trust and cultural connection high, premium at reasonable cost (₹18–30L), viseme lip-sync forgiving across languages, uncanny risk low, clearly non-human so dependency and "imitating a real person" risks drop. Abstract celestial — safest but fails the brief's requirement of eye contact/presence; retained as the low-bandwidth fallback and brand motif. *(This consciously supersedes the blueprint's abstract-only avatar: the constellation motif lives in her dupatta and the background sky.)*

**Look:** mid-30s, kind intelligent eyes, gentle smile; deep-indigo silk kurta, dupatta with faint constellation shimmer; single jhumka pair + thin bangle — elegant, never bridal/glamorous; hair loose-tied. Framing: waist-up, slightly off-centre left, eyes at 55% screen height, soft three-quarter gaze that meets camera on address. Background: slow-drifting star-field gradient (dawn palette in morning, dusk at night). Lighting: soft warm key. Expression intensity capped at 60% (no theatrical swings); head movement ±8°; blink 12–18/min randomised; lip-sync: 12-viseme set, ±80ms tolerance; gestures: hand enters frame only for namaste (welcome) and diya (night). Voice latency budget: first audio ≤ 1.2s (filler acknowledgement ≤ 400ms). Transcript: live captions word-timed under avatar; full Read-Chat view one tap. Accessibility: captions default-available, VoiceOver labels, reduced-motion mode (static portrait + waveform). Low bandwidth: static portrait + animated waveform + text.

**13 avatar states:** Welcome (namaste, warm smile) · Listening (lean-in 2°, slow nods, soft eyes) · Thinking (gaze up-left, subtle breath, "let me think" micro-tilt) · Speaking (natural visemes, eyebrow emphasis ≤ 40%) · Concern (brows soften, head tilt, NO alarm face) · Encouragement (brightened eyes, firm nod) · Celebration (full smile, small clap, one soft sparkle pass — used sparingly) · Calm guidance (stillness, slower blink) · Gentle disagreement (micro head-shake + kind smile: "I see it a little differently…") · Night reflection (dimmed palette, shawl, diya glow) · Safety escalation (motion minimised, steady eye contact, all decorative animation off) · Network delay (honest: soft "one moment…" card, never fake-thinking loop > 4s) · Mic-permission error (gestures to caption card explaining fix, no miming frustration).
