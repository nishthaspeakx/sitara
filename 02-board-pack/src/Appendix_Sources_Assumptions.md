## C. Core formulas (as implemented in Excel)
Closing customers(m) = Opening + Marketing/CAC × (1+12% referral) + Founding − Opening × churn(m) · churn(m) = max(floor, base − 0.05pt × max(0, m−12)) · Avg subscribers = (Opening+Closing)/2 · Net revenue = Avg subs × (ARPU_sub + ARPU_addon) × (1 − refund) · COGS = Avg subs × (AI+human+support+infra) + gross revenue × 2.8% · Contribution/user = ARPU_total − variable costs = ₹678 · LTV = contribution ÷ churn = ₹11,306 · Payback = CAC ÷ contribution = 5.3 months · Cash(m) = Cash(m−1) + tranches + EBITDA(m) + Δdeferred (annual plans collect ~5.5 months ahead) − capex. All 2,044 formulas recalculate live (verified zero errors).

## D. Rejected ideas and the evidence
**Consumer AI photoshoots** — Lensa: $30.75M in Dec-2022, −92% within weeks, −98% by 18 months; free Gemini "Nano Banana" served India's demand (16.6% of downloads, 1.5% of spend); child-image safety police-flagged (NCA, CyberPeace). **Celebrations/birthdays** — 1–3 events/family/yr; Evite ~$26.5M after 25 years; Minted gives planning away free. **Kitty platforms** — PartyTambola killed by Online Gaming Act 2025; KittyParty.club negligible after years. **Vastu** — zero consumer subscriptions exist anywhere; per-property pricing is revealed market wisdom (Livspace 94% one-time revenue). **Travel** — every scaled AI planner is free/affiliate (Layla, Mindtrip, GuideGeek); concierge economics are bank-funded B2B2C (Ten Lifestyle). **Standalone companionship** — the lonely person never pays (Khyaal ₹999/yr); payer channels fragile (Papa lost ~36 payers in one cycle); safety litigation active. **Personal-brand studio** — quarterly cadence; PhotoAI churn-volatile. **Life-story (Dhara)** — StoryWorth tiers end at book shipment; Artifact shut down 2025: gift-shaped, not subscription. **Broad family wellness** — HealthifyMe AI tier ₹208/mo price floor; Cult.fit ₹481 Cr FY25 loss at scale. **Weddings B2C** — 100% post-event churn; all winners monetise vendors/registry.

## E. Data limitations
Private-company revenue figures are frequently third-party estimates (GetLatka/Growjo/StarterStory) or founder-reported; AI-headshot market sizes trace to vendor marketing; Reddit could not be crawled directly in phase 2 (pains corroborated via secondary sources); no census of Indian wedding photographers exists; conservative ends of ranges used throughout.
